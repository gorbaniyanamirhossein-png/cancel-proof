What verification proves — and what it doesn't

A matching screenshot hash proves the screenshot file is the one the receipt refers to, unchanged. It does not prove the screenshot itself is genuine. A seller signature proves the holder of the matching key signed this exact receipt — trust in the key comes from the key being published at the seller's domain, not from the signature alone. A receipt is evidence the holder controls — not a court judgement.
