Make a shareable, verifiable receipt for a cancelled subscription. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Never put secrets in a receipt. No passwords, no full card or account numbers, no full email addresses. Receipts are public by design.

A receipt needs a reference number or a screenshot hash — at least one piece of evidence.

What verification proves — and what it doesn't

A matching screenshot hash proves the screenshot file is the one the receipt refers to, unchanged. It does not prove the screenshot itself is genuine. A receipt is evidence the holder controls — not a court judgement.
